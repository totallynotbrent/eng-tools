lH2 inducer sizing from tank state to blade angles: NPSH, tip diameter, speed, and head rise.

\begin{aligned} \mathrm{NPSH}_{min} &= \frac{P_1 - P_v}{\rho g}, \quad \mathrm{NPSH}_c = \frac{\mathrm{NPSH}_{min}}{1+M} \\\\ c_m &= \sqrt{\frac{2g\,\mathrm{NPSH}_c}{A}}, \quad \phi = \frac{c_m}{U_t}, \quad N = \frac{60 U_t}{\pi D_t} \end{aligned}
suction specific speeds use the huang and huzel us-unit convention: rpm, gpm, and feet of head.
