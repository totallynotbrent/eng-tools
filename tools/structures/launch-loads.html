shear, bending moment, and axial load at each segment interface from ground wind or max-q aerodynamics.

\begin{aligned} F_{y,i} &= F_{N,i}\cos\alpha - F_{D,i}\sin\alpha \\\\ V_j &= \textstyle\sum_{i\ \mathrm{above}\ j} F_{net,i}, \quad M_j = \textstyle\sum_{i\ \mathrm{above}\ j} F_{y,i}(x_{AC,i} - x_j) - F_{IR,i}(x_{GC,i} - x_j) \\\\ N_j &= a_x \textstyle\sum_{i\ \mathrm{above}\ j} m_i + \textstyle\sum_{i\ \mathrm{above}\ j} F_{x,i} \end{aligned}
